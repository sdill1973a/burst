How National Weather Service point forecasts are written. Each period is one paragraph: sky, temperature, wind, then precipitation.

Periods: Today, Tonight, This Afternoon, Overnight, then weekday names and "Monday Night".
Sky: Sunny, Mostly sunny, Partly sunny, Mostly cloudy, Cloudy, Clear, Mostly clear, Partly cloudy, Patchy fog, Areas of fog.
Weather: A chance of showers, Showers likely, Rain, A slight chance of thunderstorms, Showers and thunderstorms, Snow, A chance of rain and snow, Isolated showers, Scattered showers and thunderstorms, Drizzle, Freezing rain.
Timing: before 1pm, after 10am, between 7pm and 1am, then, mainly after 4pm.
Temperature: with a high near 72. with a low around 48. Highs in the mid 60s. Lows in the upper 30s. Temperature falling to around 50 by 5am. Temperature rising to around 55 after 1am.
Wind: Calm wind. Light and variable wind. Southwest wind around 5 mph. Northwest wind 10 to 15 mph, with gusts as high as 25 mph. South wind 5 to 10 mph becoming calm in the evening. East wind 10 to 15 mph increasing to 20 to 25 mph in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 35 mph.
Precipitation: Chance of precipitation is 30%. Chance of precipitation is 80%. New rainfall amounts of less than a tenth of an inch possible. New rainfall amounts between a tenth and quarter of an inch possible. New rainfall amounts between a half and three quarters of an inch possible. New snow accumulation of 1 to 2 inches possible. Little or no snow accumulation expected.

Example:
Tonight: A chance of showers, mainly after 2am. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 51. South wind 5 to 10 mph. Chance of precipitation is 40%. New rainfall amounts of less than a tenth of an inch possible.
